# TikTok Shop Product Scorecard — Scoring Framework

## The 10 Categories (each scored 1-10)

### Categories 1-5: The Hook & Core Appeal
These determine whether a product can go viral in the first place.

| # | Category | Key Question | Score 10 = | Score 1 = |
|---|----------|-------------|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | Hook Potential | Can it grab attention in 3-5 seconds? | Instant visual transformation or shock | Boring, requires explanation |
| 2 | Problem Solving Strength | Clear, emotional pain point? | Universal daily frustration solved visibly | No real problem or niche pain |
| 3 | Demonstrability on Video | One clean shot demo? | Product does the selling in 6 seconds | Needs long explanation or setup |
| 4 | Unique Selling Proposition | Distinct mechanism/design/angle? | Never-seen-before mechanism | Generic commodity, many identical options |
| 5 | Creator Compatibility | Will creators WANT to film it? | Creators request samples proactively | Hard to make interesting content |

### Categories 6-10: Scalability & Operations
These decide whether a winner sustains traction or stalls after week one.

| # | Category | Key Question | Score 10 = | Score 1 = |
|---|----------|-------------|-----------|-----------|
| 6 | Content Angle Depth | 10+ angles, hooks, case studies? | Infinite use cases and audiences | One angle, one audience, done |
| 7 | Price Point & Margin Structure | Impulse price + affiliate/ad room? | Sub-$30, 60%+ margin, 20%+ commission viable | Expensive, thin margin, no affiliate room |
| 8 | Operational Simplicity | Easy fulfill, low returns, FBT? | Lightweight, durable, no setup, FBT-ready | Fragile, heavy, complex setup, high return rate |
| 9 | Emotional & Social Proof Potential | Comment section explode? | Built-in "this is genius" reaction | No emotional response, functional only |
| 10 | Market Timing & Saturation | Category still scalable? | Whitespace, early mover advantage | Oversaturated, dozens of competitors |

## Score Bands & Verdicts

| Score | Verdict | Action |
|-------|---------|--------|
| 80-100 | Aggressively Pursue | Greenlight full creator + ad rollout. Product, price, pain point all line up. |
| 65-79 | Strong w/ Proper Positioning | Move forward with focused creative angle. Watch CPMs/CVR; double down after signal. |
| 50-64 | Needs Better Hooks | Rework positioning, packaging, hooks, or pricing before spending on creators. |
| Under 50 | Hard to Scale | Walk away or rework the product. Fundamentals are wrong — TTS scale will burn cash. |

## Scoring Tips

- **Be honest, not optimistic.** The scorecard exists to prevent emotional spending.
- **Score relative to TikTok Shop specifically**, not general e-commerce viability.
- **Market Timing is the hardest to score** — check FastMoss/Kalodata for category saturation.
- **A 7 is strong.** Reserve 9-10 for truly exceptional performance in that category.
- **Low scores aren't death sentences** for categories 6-10 — they're fixable with strategy.
