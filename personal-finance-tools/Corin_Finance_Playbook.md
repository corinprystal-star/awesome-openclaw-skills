# Corin’s Personal Finance Playbook

**The Goal:** Stop the bleeding, gain cash flow visibility, and systematically destroy debt.

*Built specifically for your ADHD brain: Scannable, visual, actionable, and structured.*

---

## 🛑 Phase 1: Triage (Stop the Bleeding)

You can't fix cash flow if you don't know where the leaks are.

### 1. The "Zero-Based" Audit
* **Action:** Review your last 30 days of transactions in Monarch.
* **Focus:** Subscriptions, recurring app charges, Amazon impulse buys, and "convenience" spending (UberEats, DoorDash).
* **Rule:** If it doesn't keep you alive, employed, or sane, pause it for 30 days. You can always turn it back on.

### 2. The "Bills Account" Strategy
* **Action:** Open a secondary checking account (if you don't already have one).
* **Focus:** Separation of funds.
* **Rule:** 
    * **Account A (Bills):** ONLY for fixed, recurring expenses (Rent, utilities, debt minimums, insurance).
    * **Account B (Life):** For variable spending (Groceries, gas, fun).
    * *Why this works for ADHD:* If Account B is empty, you're done spending. You never accidentally spend rent money on dinner.

---

## 💸 Phase 2: Cash Flow Visibility

We need to know exactly when money enters and leaves.

### 1. The 50/30/20 Framework (Adjusted for High Debt)
* **50% Needs:** Rent, utilities, groceries, minimum debt payments.
* **30% Debt Destruction:** (Usually "Wants", but we are shifting this to aggressively attack debt).
* **20% Savings/Buffer:** Emergency fund (Start with $1,000, then build to 3-6 months).

### 2. The Weekly "Money Date"
* **Action:** Set a 15-minute recurring calendar invite (e.g., Sunday morning).
* **Focus:** Review Monarch Money.
* **Checklist:**
    * [ ] Categorize new transactions.
    * [ ] Check budget vs. actuals.
    * [ ] Identify upcoming bills for the week.

---

## ⚔️ Phase 3: Debt Destruction

### The Avalanche vs. Snowball Strategy

* **The Snowball Method (Recommended for ADHD):**
    * **How it works:** Pay minimums on everything. Put every extra dollar toward the **smallest balance** debt.
    * **Why it works:** You get quick wins. The dopamine hit of closing an account keeps you motivated.

* **The Avalanche Method (The Math Approach):**
    * **How it works:** Pay minimums on everything. Put every extra dollar toward the **highest interest rate** debt.
    * **Why it works:** It saves you the most money in interest over time.

**Action:** List all your debts in Monarch. Decide which method gives you the mental energy to keep going. If you need the quick win, do Snowball. If you hate paying interest, do Avalanche.
