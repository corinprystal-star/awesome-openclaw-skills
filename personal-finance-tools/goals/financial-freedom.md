## Objective

Achieve positive monthly cash flow, build a $1,000 emergency fund, and systematically eliminate all consumer debt within 12-18 months.

## Why This Matters

Without cash flow control, every month is a crisis. Debt creates a constant background stress that makes it impossible to focus on career growth, enjoy life, or build toward bigger goals. Financial stability is the foundation everything else sits on.

## Success Criteria

- [ ] All bills paid on time for 3 consecutive months
- [ ] $1,000 emergency fund established
- [ ] Monthly spending tracked and categorized consistently
- [ ] At least one debt account fully paid off
- [ ] Positive cash flow (income > expenses) every month for 6 months
- [ ] Total debt reduced by 30%+ from starting balance

## Action Plan

### Phase 1: Triage (Week 1-2)
1. Connect all accounts to Monarch Money
2. Run full financial audit (accounts, debts, recurring)
3. Cancel/pause unnecessary subscriptions
4. Set up Bills Account separation (if not done)
5. Establish baseline numbers for all metrics

### Phase 2: Stabilize (Month 1-2)
1. Set up budgets in Monarch for all categories
2. Implement Weekly Money Date habit
3. Pay all bills on time (set up autopay where possible)
4. Start $1,000 emergency fund ($100-250/month)
5. Identify one quick-win debt to attack first

### Phase 3: Attack (Month 3-12)
1. Apply Snowball or Avalanche method
2. Find additional income opportunities (freelance, side gig)
3. Monthly debt payoff progress reviews
4. Celebrate each debt paid off

### Phase 4: Build (Month 12-18)
1. Grow emergency fund to 3 months expenses
2. Start investing (even $50/month)
3. Maintain positive cash flow habits

## Potential Obstacles

- **ADHD tax (forgetting to check):** Mitigate with automated reminders and Weekly Money Date habit
- **Impulse spending:** Mitigate with Bills Account separation and hard spending cap
- **Income instability (job transition):** Mitigate with emergency fund and side income
- **Motivation drop:** Mitigate with Snowball method quick wins and visual progress tracking
- **Unexpected expenses:** Mitigate with emergency fund (even $500 helps)
