## The Rules

1. **If you forgot you had it → CANCEL**
2. **If you haven't used it in 30 days → PAUSE**
3. **If it sparks joy AND you use it weekly → KEEP**
4. **If it's a "maybe someday" → CANCEL (you can always re-subscribe)**
